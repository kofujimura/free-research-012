## 2. Corrections and changes relative to the original manuscript

- **Numbers: none changed.** `preprint/make_tables_tex.py` rebuilds every table with the run's own `scripts/render_paper.py` functions from the analysis CSVs listed in `config/report-results.json`, re-renders `paper.template.md`, and asserts that the result is byte-identical to `paper.md`. The analysis CSVs store unrounded values (no rounding calls in the analysis scripts), and the renderer rounds once for display, so no rounding corrections were needed (unlike free-research-004). `preprint/check_values.py` confirms that all 389 interval triples in the English version occur verbatim in `paper.md`, except the eight figure values below.
- **Figures replaced by tables** (author's preference). `figures/national-model-information.*`: its left panel repeats Table 1; its middle and right panels (ratios against the fixed six-method ensemble and past-performance selection for information ages 0, 1 and 2 weeks) were tabulated in the original only for age 1. Table 8 of the English version adds the ages 0 and 2, read from `experiments/E022-extra-analysis-fixed/results/comparisons.csv` with the same filters as `scripts/plot_results.py`: Chronos-Bolt vs. fixed 6 0.995 [0.900, 1.078] and 1.238 [1.102, 1.364]; TimesFM vs. fixed 6 0.918 [0.856, 0.977] and 1.249 [1.144, 1.365]; Chronos-Bolt vs. past selection 0.885 [0.820, 0.968] and 1.103 [1.005, 1.240]; TimesFM vs. past selection 0.813 [0.734, 0.916] and 1.105 [0.991, 1.266]. `figures/generalization-one-week.*` repeats Tables 1, 8 and 13 and was omitted; its caption's cautions were moved into the caption of Table 13. The original figures remain in `figures/`.
- **Format only:** the Japanese abstract was translated into an abstract of at most 1,920 characters, and its results were also listed as "Summary of findings" in the Introduction. Internal file paths were replaced by "the replication package". The bibliography gained DOIs or URLs for every entry.
- **References re-checked** on 2026-10-19 (`preprint/sources_check/`): the four arXiv references through the arXiv API, the ten DOIs through Crossref, and the eleven other URLs by HTTP (all resolved; status 200). No reference was removed. Kalahasti et al. (2025) remains cited for its existence and abstract only, as in the original. Alhassan et al. now has a v2 on arXiv; the paper cites v1 as read by the run.

- **AI Declaration** added (model, session, budgets, interventions, tools, verification, conflicts of interest, authorship). The research model's exact identifier is not in the run's records; the English version gives it as stated by the human author (§4), and a footnote to the fifth limitation says so.
- **Data attribution** (2026-10-19, after reading the data providers' terms). The original manuscript named the providers and the licence but not the required New Zealand attribution or the citations the providers recommend.
